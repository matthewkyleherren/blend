About Us

Born from a lifelong passion for travel and the world's diverse coffee cultures, we are a family-owned and run coffee shop, newly founded in 2025 right here on the beautiful Isle of Wight.

Our journey began years ago, exploring culinary and coffee experiences across the globe. We’ve poured those memories, flavours, and lessons into creating a welcoming space for you in Ryde.

You'll find us right on the Ryde Esplanade, perfectly situated directly opposite the Hovercraft terminal, close to both Wight link Catamaran and Island Rail links, It’s the ideal spot to grab a coffee before your journey or settle in for a relaxed afternoon.

Our Local Heart

How we do it

We believe the best coffee starts locally. We are proud to partner with the island's leading roastery, the IOW Espresso Company, to bring you a truly exceptional cup every time.

From our daily baked cakes and fresh/made to order savoury options to our seasonal breakfast and Brunch/lunch menus, our food is crafted with the same commitment to quality.
